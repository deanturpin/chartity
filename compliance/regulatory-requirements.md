# Compliance and Regulatory Requirements

## 1. Overview

This document outlines the legal and regulatory compliance requirements for
fundraising organisations in the UK, providing a framework for maintaining
legal status and avoiding regulatory issues.

## 2. Charity Commission Requirements

### 2.1 Registration Thresholds

**Charitable Purposes Test:**

- Organisation must exist for exclusively charitable purposes
- Public benefit requirement must be met
- Activities must align with charitable objectives

**Income Thresholds:**

- **Under £5,000 annually:** No registration required (exempt charity)
- **£5,000 - £100,000:** Must register with Charity Commission
- **Over £100,000:** Annual return and accounts filing required

### 2.2 Annual Compliance

**Annual Return (AR30):**

- [ ] Submit by 31 January each year
- [ ] Update trustee information
- [ ] Confirm charitable activities
- [ ] Provide financial summary

**Annual Accounts:**

- [ ] **Under £25,000:** Receipts and payments account
- [ ] **£25,000 - £250,000:** Independent examination required
- [ ] **Over £250,000:** Full audit required

## 3. Fundraising Regulation

### 3.1 Fundraising Regulator Standards

**Registration Requirements:**

- [ ] Register if fundraising income exceeds £100,000 annually
- [ ] Comply with Code of Fundraising Practice
- [ ] Display registration number on materials

**Key Standards:**

- Honest and truthful fundraising
- Respect for donor choice and privacy
- Protection of vulnerable people
- Transparent use of funds

### 3.2 Local Authority Licensing

**Street Collections:**

- [ ] Apply for street collection permit
- [ ] Specify dates, times, and locations
- [ ] Provide collector identification
- [ ] Submit collection returns

**House-to-House Collections:**

- [ ] Obtain licence from local authority
- [ ] Background checks for collectors
- [ ] Issue collection certificates
- [ ] Maintain collection records

**Lotteries and Raffles:**

- [ ] Register small society lotteries
- [ ] External lottery manager licensing
- [ ] Prize limits and ticket regulations
- [ ] Return submissions

## 4. Data Protection (GDPR)

### 4.1 Legal Basis for Processing

**Donor Data Processing:**

- [ ] Legitimate interest for fundraising activities
- [ ] Consent for marketing communications
- [ ] Legal obligation for Gift Aid records
- [ ] Document lawful basis decisions

### 4.2 Data Protection Requirements

**Privacy Notices:**

- [ ] Clear explanation of data use
- [ ] Retention periods specified
- [ ] Rights of data subjects explained
- [ ] Contact details for queries

**Data Security:**

- [ ] Appropriate technical measures
- [ ] Staff training on data handling
- [ ] Regular security assessments
- [ ] Incident response procedures

## 5. Financial Compliance

### 5.1 HMRC Requirements

**Gift Aid:**

- [ ] Maintain donor declarations
- [ ] Submit quarterly claims
- [ ] Annual declaration to HMRC
- [ ] Record keeping for 6 years

**VAT Registration:**

- [ ] Monitor annual turnover threshold (£85,000)
- [ ] Register if threshold exceeded
- [ ] Submit quarterly returns
- [ ] Maintain VAT records

### 5.2 Anti-Money Laundering

**Due Diligence Requirements:**

- [ ] Customer due diligence for large donations
- [ ] Enhanced due diligence for high-risk donors
- [ ] Ongoing monitoring of donor relationships
- [ ] Suspicious activity reporting

## 6. Employment Law (If Applicable)

### 6.1 Staff Employment

**Legal Requirements:**

- [ ] Employment contracts

- [ ] Health and safety obligations

### 6.2 Volunteer Management

**Best Practices:**

## 7. Insurance Requirements

### 7.1 Essential Insurance

**Public Liability:**

- [ ] Minimum £1 million coverage

**Professional Indemnity:**

- [ ] Cover for advice giving activities

- [ ] Legal expenses cover
- [ ] Cyber liability protection

## 8. Reporting and Filing Calendar

### 8.1 Annual Deadlines

| Date | Requirement | Authority |
|------|-------------|-----------|
| 31 January | Annual Return (AR30) | Charity Commission |
| 31 January | Annual Accounts | Charity Commission |
| 31 January | Corporation Tax Return | HMRC |
| Quarterly | VAT Returns (if registered) | HMRC |
| Quarterly | Gift Aid Claims | HMRC |

### 8.2 Event-Based Requirements

**Within 3 Months:**

- [ ] Report serious incidents to Charity Commission
- [ ] Notify changes to trustee details
- [ ] Update registered address changes

**Within 12 Months:**

- [ ] File accounts after year-end
- [ ] Submit independent examiner's report
- [ ] Complete annual governance review

# Fundraising Policies and Procedures

## 1. Fundraising Ethics and Standards

### 1.1 Core Principles

- All fundraising activities must be honest, transparent, and ethical
- Donors must be fully informed about how their contributions will be used
- No misleading statements or false representations permitted
- Respect for donor privacy and preferences

### 1.2 Prohibited Activities

- Door-to-door solicitation without proper authorisation
- Aggressive or pressurised fundraising tactics
- Misrepresentation of organisation purpose or fund usage
- Fundraising for personal benefit

## 2. Types of Fundraising Activities

### 2.1 Approved Fundraising Methods

- **Events and Activities:**
  - Charity walks/runs
  - Bake sales and food events
  - Raffles and prize draws (subject to licensing)
  - Sponsored activities
  - Community events

- **Direct Fundraising:**
  - Online crowdfunding platforms
  - Social media campaigns
  - Email fundraising (with consent)
  - Workplace giving schemes

### 2.2 Activity Approval Process

1. Submit fundraising proposal to leadership team
2. Review compliance with legal requirements
3. Assess resource requirements and potential risks
4. Obtain necessary permits or licences
5. Document approval and proceed with planning

## 3. Legal and Regulatory Compliance

### 3.1 Licensing Requirements

- **Raffles and Lotteries:** Obtain appropriate licence from local authority
- **Street Collections:** Apply for street collection permit
- **House-to-House Collections:** Secure proper authorisation
- **Professional Fundraising:** Register with relevant regulatory bodies

### 3.2 Data Protection

- Obtain proper consent for marketing communications
- Secure storage and handling of donor information
- Respect donor preferences for communication
- Regular data protection compliance reviews

## 4. Donation Processing

### 4.1 Acceptable Payment Methods

- Cash (with proper receipt and recording)
- Cheques made payable to organisation
- Bank transfers to organisation account
- Card payments through authorised systems
- Online payment platforms (PayPal, Stripe, etc.)

### 4.2 Donation Recording

- All donations must be recorded immediately
- Issue receipts for all donations over £10
- Maintain donor database with contact preferences
- Regular reconciliation of donations received

### 4.3 Gift Aid

- Offer Gift Aid declarations to eligible UK taxpayers
- Maintain proper Gift Aid records
- Submit Gift Aid claims to HMRC as appropriate
- Provide donors with Gift Aid information

## 5. Financial Procedures

### 5.1 Cash Handling

- Count cash donations in presence of two people
- Immediate recording and secure storage
- Daily banking of cash receipts where possible
- Maintain cash handling logs

### 5.2 Banking Procedures

- All donations banked within 48 hours
- Use organisation bank account only
- Record bank references for all deposits
- Monthly bank reconciliation

## 6. Expense Management

### 6.1 Fundraising Costs

- Keep fundraising costs to reasonable percentage of funds raised
- Pre-approval required for all fundraising expenses
- Maintain receipts for all expenditure
- Regular review of cost-effectiveness

### 6.2 Expense Categories

- **Direct Costs:** Materials, venue hire, licences
- **Administrative Costs:** Banking fees, insurance, communications
- **Equipment:** Reusable items for multiple events

## 7. Donor Relations

### 7.1 Donor Communication

- Thank all donors promptly
- Provide updates on fund usage and impact
- Respect donor communication preferences
- Handle complaints promptly and professionally

### 7.2 Donor Recognition

- Public recognition only with donor consent
- Maintain donor anonymity when requested
- Consider donor recognition events or materials
- Balance recognition with privacy requirements

## 8. Risk Management

### 8.1 Event Risk Assessment

- Complete risk assessment for all fundraising events
- Obtain appropriate insurance coverage
- Ensure adequate supervision and safety measures
- Have emergency procedures in place

### 8.2 Financial Risk Management

- Set realistic fundraising targets
- Monitor expenses against budget
- Regular financial reviews
- Contingency planning for poor performance

## 9. Reporting and Evaluation

### 9.1 Activity Reporting

- Document outcomes of all fundraising activities
- Track costs versus funds raised
- Evaluate effectiveness and lessons learned
- Report to stakeholders and authorities as required

### 9.2 Annual Review

- Complete annual review of fundraising performance
- Assess compliance with policies and procedures
- Update policies based on experience and regulatory changes
- Set targets and priorities for following year

## 10. Training and Responsibilities

### 10.1 Volunteer Training

- Provide training on fundraising ethics and procedures
- Ensure volunteers understand legal requirements
- Regular updates on policy changes
- Clear guidelines on authority and limitations

### 10.2 Accountability

- Designated person responsible for fundraising oversight
- Regular monitoring of compliance
- Clear escalation procedures for issues
- Documentation of all decisions and approvals

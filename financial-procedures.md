# Financial Management and Accounting Procedures

## 1. Financial Management Framework

### 1.1 Core Principles
- **Transparency:** All financial activities must be transparent and accountable
- **Segregation of Duties:** No single person controls all aspects of financial transactions
- **Regular Monitoring:** Continuous oversight and regular reporting
- **Compliance:** Adherence to all applicable laws and regulations
- **Documentation:** Comprehensive record-keeping for audit trail

### 1.2 Financial Objectives
- Maximise funds available for charitable purposes
- Minimise administrative costs while maintaining proper controls
- Ensure legal compliance and regulatory requirements
- Provide clear accountability to donors and stakeholders

## 2. Banking and Account Management

### 2.1 Bank Account Structure
- **Primary Operating Account:** Main account for all transactions
- **Savings Account:** For holding reserves and surplus funds
- **Account Signatories:** Minimum two required for all transactions over £100

### 2.2 Banking Procedures
- **Daily Banking:** Deposit all cash receipts within 24-48 hours
- **Monthly Reconciliation:** Complete bank reconciliation by 10th of following month
- **Signatory Management:** Review and update authorised signatories annually
- **Online Banking:** Secure access with appropriate user permissions

### 2.3 Cash Management
- **Petty Cash:** Maximum £200 float for small expenses
- **Cash Counting:** Two-person verification for all cash handling
- **Cash Security:** Secure storage in locked facility when not banked
- **Cash Limits:** Maximum £500 cash at any event or location

## 3. Income Management

### 3.1 Donation Processing
- **Receipt of Donations:**
  - Issue receipt for all donations over £10
  - Record immediately in donation register
  - Bank within 48 hours of receipt
  - Update donor database

- **Gift Aid Processing:**
  - Collect Gift Aid declarations from eligible donors
  - Maintain Gift Aid database
  - Submit quarterly claims to HMRC
  - Monitor annual donation limits

### 3.2 Event Income
- **Pre-Event Planning:**
  - Set income targets and budgets
  - Designate cash handling responsibilities
  - Prepare income recording systems

- **During Events:**
  - Use cash boxes with initial float
  - Record all transactions immediately
  - Count and verify takings with witnesses
  - Secure cash storage during events

- **Post-Event:**
  - Final count and reconciliation
  - Complete income summary report
  - Bank all proceeds within 24 hours

## 4. Expenditure Control

### 4.1 Authorisation Levels
- **Under £50:** Treasurer approval
- **£50-£200:** Two signatory approval
- **£200-£500:** Committee approval in writing
- **Over £500:** Formal meeting approval with minutes

### 4.2 Purchase Procedures
- **Purchase Authorisation:**
  - Complete purchase request form
  - Obtain appropriate approval level
  - Retain copies of all documentation

- **Payment Processing:**
  - Original receipts required for all payments
  - Cheque payments require two signatures
  - Bank transfer approvals documented
  - Petty cash reimbursements properly documented

### 4.3 Expense Categories
- **Fundraising Expenses:** Direct costs of fundraising activities
- **Administrative Expenses:** General operational costs
- **Programme Expenses:** Direct charitable programme costs
- **Governance Expenses:** Meeting costs, legal fees, compliance

## 5. Record-Keeping and Documentation

### 5.1 Financial Records
- **Cash Book:** Record all income and expenditure transactions
- **Bank Records:** Maintain bank statements and reconciliations
- **Receipt Files:** Organised filing system for all supporting documents
- **Donor Database:** Comprehensive record of all donations and donors

### 5.2 Document Retention
- **Financial Records:** Retain for minimum 7 years
- **Bank Statements:** Keep all original statements
- **Receipts and Invoices:** File chronologically with cross-references
- **Meeting Minutes:** Permanent retention of all financial decisions

### 5.3 Backup and Security
- **Digital Backups:** Regular backup of electronic records
- **Physical Security:** Fireproof storage for critical documents
- **Access Control:** Limited access to sensitive financial information
- **Data Protection:** Compliance with GDPR for donor information

## 6. Financial Reporting

### 6.1 Monthly Reporting
- **Income and Expenditure Statement:** Monthly summary by category
- **Bank Reconciliation:** Comparison of book and bank balances
- **Cash Flow Statement:** Analysis of cash position and projections
- **Variance Analysis:** Comparison against budget and previous periods

### 6.2 Annual Reporting
- **Annual Accounts:** Complete financial statements
- **Independent Examination:** External review of accounts
- **Annual Return:** Submission to relevant regulatory bodies
- **Donor Report:** Summary for stakeholders and supporters

### 6.3 Internal Controls
- **Monthly Reviews:** Leadership team review of financial position
- **Quarterly Assessments:** Detailed analysis of financial performance
- **Annual Audit:** Independent examination of financial procedures
- **Exception Reporting:** Immediate reporting of significant variances

## 7. Budget Management

### 7.1 Budget Preparation
- **Annual Budget:** Comprehensive income and expenditure projections
- **Event Budgets:** Specific budgets for fundraising activities
- **Contingency Planning:** Reserve funds and risk mitigation
- **Regular Review:** Quarterly budget reviews and updates

### 7.2 Budget Monitoring
- **Monthly Variance Reports:** Actual versus budget analysis
- **Forecast Updates:** Regular revision of annual projections
- **Approval Process:** Changes to budget require formal approval
- **Performance Indicators:** Key metrics for financial performance

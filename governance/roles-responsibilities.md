# Governance Structure and Role Definitions

## 1. Organisational Structure

### 1.1 Governance Hierarchy

```text
Organisation Structure:

Founding Members
      ↓
Board of Trustees/Committee
      ↓
Executive Officers
      ↓
Volunteers and Supporters
```

### 1.2 Decision-Making Authority

**Strategic Decisions:** Board/Committee consensus
**Operational Decisions:** Executive officers
**Financial Decisions:** Treasurer + one other signatory
**Emergency Decisions:** Chairperson (with subsequent ratification)

## 2. Board of Trustees/Committee

### 2.1 Composition

**Minimum Members:** 3 trustees
**Maximum Members:** 12 trustees
**Term Length:** 3 years (renewable)
**Meeting Frequency:** Quarterly minimum

### 2.2 Collective Responsibilities

**Legal Duties:**

- Ensure organisation compliance with law
- Act in best interests of beneficiaries
- Manage resources responsibly
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Ensure accountability and transparency

**Strategic Oversight:**

- Set organisational strategy and priorities
- Monitor performance against objectives
- Approve annual budgets and major expenditure
- Oversee risk management
- Ensure effective governance

## 5. Role Appointment Process

### 5.1 Recruitment

**Internal Recruitment:**

- Open to existing volunteers and supporters
- Skills-based selection criteria
- Application and interview process
- Reference checks where appropriate

**External Recruitment:**

- Advertise through relevant networks
- Volunteer recruitment websites
- Professional networks and contacts
- Skills-based recruitment campaigns

### 5.2 Appointment Procedure

**Nomination Process:**

1. **Self-nomination** or nomination by existing member
2. **Application** including skills and experience summary
3. **Interview** with existing committee members
4. **References** from previous roles or activities
5. **Appointment** by committee vote

**Approval Requirements:**

- Simple majority vote for most positions
- Two-thirds majority for chair and treasurer
- Formal appointment at next meeting
- Confirmation in meeting minutes

## 8. Conflict Resolution

### 8.1 Internal Disputes

**Resolution Process:**

1. **Direct Discussion:** Attempt informal resolution
2. **Mediation:** Chair or neutral member facilitates
3. **Formal Review:** Committee consideration
4. **External Mediation:** Independent advisor if needed
5. **Final Decision:** Formal committee vote

### 8.2 Conflict of Interest

**Management Procedures:**

- [ ] Declaration of interests register
- [ ] Annual conflict review
- [ ] Meeting declarations required
- [ ] Withdrawal from relevant decisions
- [ ] Documentation of conflict management

## 9. Committee Meetings

### 9.1 Meeting Structure

**Regular Meetings:**

- **Frequency:** Quarterly minimum
- **Duration:** 2-3 hours maximum
- **Agenda:** Circulated 7 days in advance
- **Minutes:** Distributed within 14 days
- **Quorum:** 50% of committee members

### 9.2 Decision Making

**Voting Procedures:**

- Simple majority for routine decisions
- Two-thirds majority for constitutional changes
- Unanimous consent for major policy changes
- Chair has casting vote in tied decisions
- Proxy voting not permitted
